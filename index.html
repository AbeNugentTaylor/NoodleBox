How to play

  • Press ▶ to start the clock (and the sound).
  • Drag between two jacks to run a cable — outputs on the right of a module, inputs on the left. Matching colors connect (● signals, ● triggers).
  • Click a cable to snip it. Drag a plugged input jack to unplug it.
  • Drag knobs up/down to turn them (hold shift for fine control), double-click to reset.
  • On the sequencer: click a step to mute/unmute it, drag a step up/down to change its note.
  • Drag a module by its title to move it; × removes it.

Ideas: envelope → amp cv makes plucks · lfo → filter cut makes wobble · a sequencer's gate into another sequencer's clock chains rhythms · cables into anything, there are no wrong answers.
