How to play

  • Press ▶ to start the clock (and the sound). Tap + module to add one — pick a category, then the module.
  • Drag from a jack to run a cable — outputs on the right of a module, inputs on the left. Every jack that cable could actually feed lights up as you drag; everything else fades back, so you can't land on a dead end. Prefer tapping? Tap a jack, then tap the (lit-up) jack you want to land on — tap the same jack again to cancel. Either way, tapping a jack (without moving) shows what it does.
  • Click a cable to snip it. An input with a single cable can be dragged straight off to rewire it; an input already carrying several (fan several drums into one speaker, say) just gets another when you drag to it — click the specific cable you want to remove instead.
  • Drag knobs up/down to turn them (hold shift for fine control), double-click to reset.
  • On the sequencer: click a step to mute/unmute it, drag a step up/down to change its note (the scale dropdown constrains dragged notes to a chosen scale/chord). The steps knob sets the pattern length — raise it above 8 for longer patterns, or give two tracks different lengths for polyrhythmic drift. An unconnected sequencer shows a sound preset dropdown (Strings, Pluck, Pad, Drums) that auto-builds a full oscillator + envelope + filter chain behind it.
  • A clock module's gate output, cabled into several sequencers'/arpeggios' clock inputs, syncs them to one master beat — each track's own rate dropdown (÷8…x8) then runs it at a multiple or fraction of that beat.
  • A sequencer or arpeggio with no oscillator (or other sound source) anywhere downstream in its signal chain shows a ⚠ next to its title — tap it for details. It's just a flag; nothing is blocked.
  • Drag a module by its title to move it; × removes it. A module with a swap dropdown next to its title can be replaced with a compatible one (e.g. delay for reverb, sequencer for arpeggio) without rewiring its cables.
  • The board grows to fit wherever you place modules, so there's no fixed edge to run out of. Use the −/+ in the corner to zoom the whole board out or in, or tap fit to zoom out just far enough to see the whole patch at once.
  • No sound? Something needs to be cabled into the speaker, and on iPhone the silent switch mutes web audio — flip it and raise the media volume.

Ideas: envelope → amp cv makes plucks · lfo → filter cut makes wobble · a sequencer's gate into another sequencer's clock chains rhythms.
